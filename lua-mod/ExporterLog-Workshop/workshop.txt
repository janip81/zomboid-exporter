description=Server-side-only mod that logs extra player stats to Logs/logs_*/*_ExporterLog.txt for external stats tracking via a companion Prometheus exporter. Tracks zombie kills (with cause: melee/firearm/vehicle, plus weapon or vehicle type), driving distance and max speed (per vehicle type), walking/running/sprinting distance, eating, drinking (from inventory or a world source like a sink/toilet/lake, including which one), pills/medicine taken, books read, vehicle enter/exit, medical treatment (bandaging/disinfecting/stitching/splinting, self or others), and injuries (bites/scratches/lacerations/burns/fractures, per body part). No client-side code -- has zero effect on players who don't have it, since everything runs on the dedicated server process itself.
